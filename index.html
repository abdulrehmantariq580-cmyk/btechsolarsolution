What is a Hybrid System?

The perfect balance for Pakistan. It connects to the Grid and includes Batteries. During the day, it powers your home and exports extra energy (Net Metering), while batteries provide seamless backup during load-shedding.

Designed strictly for maximizing savings. It connects directly to the Grid without batteries. Ideal for Zeroing out your bills through Net Metering, though it shuts down during grid power outages for safety.
